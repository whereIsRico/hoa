Meet Palisade.

The gate and guest management platform built for Bahamian HOAs. Residents clear their guests ahead of time, gate staff check them in from a tablet, and the board gets a record that can't be rewritten.

  • One record, three views Residents, gate staff, and the board each see what they need, all writing to the same log.
  • An audit trail that holds up Every entry is timestamped by the system, not written by hand after the fact.
